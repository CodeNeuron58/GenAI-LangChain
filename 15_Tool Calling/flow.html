📝 Message History Flow

STEP 1: Initial Query
messages = 1 item
👤 Human
"Can you multiply 3 with 1000?"
↓
STEP 2: LLM Responds

result = llm_with_tools.invoke(messages)

🤖 AI
"I will use the multiply tool..."
+ Tool Call: multiply(a=3, b=1000)
↓
STEP 3: Append to History

messages.append(result)

messages = 2 items
👤 Human
"Can you multiply 3 with 1000?"
🤖 AI
"I will use the multiply tool..."
+ Tool Call: multiply(a=3, b=1000)
💡 Why append the AI response?

The LLM needs to see the complete conversation history every time you call it. By appending the AI's response to the messages list, the next interaction will have full context of what was discussed and what tools were called.

🔑 Think of it as:

A conversation transcript that grows with each message. Without it, the AI would have amnesia and forget what it just said!
